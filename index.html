Common Inquiries

Clear answers regarding workstation reservations, laboratory time windows, and account verification requirements.

Workstation Booking

How does the PC allocation and booking process work?

Students create an account, complete the 6-digit email verification, and log in to the student dashboard. From there, you can view live PC slot availability, select an available workstation terminal, and submit your research model requirements and supervisor endorsement for administrator approval.

Time Slots & Schedules

What are the available daily laboratory time slots?

Workstation access is structured into three fixed, non-overlapping windows daily: 09:00 – 11:00, 11:00 – 13:00, and 14:00 – 16:00. This ensures equitable GPU scheduling across ongoing deep learning, simulation, and thesis projects.

Account Requirements

What information is required to register an account?

Registration requires an official university Student ID (e.g. ST04 or CSE 033 08432), university email address, phone number, academic department, and graduation batch year. Every new account is secured through a mandatory 6-digit email verification step prior to activation.

Hardware & Search

Can I filter workstations by GPU models or RAM capacity?

Yes. The student dashboard features a live search bar within the PC Slot Availability grid. You can filter workstation terminals by hardware specifications like RTX 4090, RTX A5000, RAM capacity (16GB/8GB), or processor tier before selecting a slot.
